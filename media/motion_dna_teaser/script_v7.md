# Motion DNA teaser · script v7 (draft for review, ~66 s)

Built on Gian's v6 and the direction from leadership (Oct 12): Motion DNA is **a motion MCP that helps you make better
animation**. The designer decides and directs; the AI accelerates. Never "the AI does it alone": no "Claude", no
"AI animation", no "your AI animates", no "one click", no "open source".

**Frame:** the whole piece is a 1985 TV infomercial on air. Location changes are channel flips / VHS tracking glitches
(static, a channel number in the corner). The cheap studio in scene 10 is the infomercial's own set.
**Threads:** the Motion DNA box travels from hand to hand; Dana goes from frustrated to directing; Marcus from asleep to
scared to sceptical to "YES!"; bookend: the same fade on Dana's CRT opens the film and, transformed, closes it.
**Music:** dialogue scenes hold 5+ beats; the pillars and "But wait, there's more" cut on the beat; the logo lands on a
downbeat (Motion Style Map, research/styles/_music_and_edit.md).
