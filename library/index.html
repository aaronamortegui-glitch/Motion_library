Motion DNA

Motion made with AI all looks the same. Motion DNA is a library of moves, styles, techniques and a kit of pieces Claude builds for you, tagged by energy (1 calm → 5 explosive) and tone, so you or Claude can pick, mix and grow an identity. Everything is native keyframes and expressions.

Styles

A whole comp in one click: each layer gets the preset of its role, with the style's own curve. Preview: title · text · shapes · media.

Techniques

Script workflows that leave a technique in every project. Each one says what it needs and what to ask Claude.

The kit

Pieces Claude builds on the open comp through the MCP server, on the same token curves, in families: one card per family, its variants as chips (hover a card to play it, click a chip to see that variant). Ask for any variant by name; the call is on each card.
