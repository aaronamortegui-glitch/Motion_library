AI can now drive After Effects

New LLM capabilities can speed up animation and control software like After Effects efficiently. Three gaps still kept it out of real client work.

Same moves

Alone, an LLM keys everything linearly, fades it all in at once, holds still and cuts. AI motion all looks the same.

Code only

Many AI motion tools only output code or a rendered clip. Client work needs layers and keyframes in After Effects that a team can still edit.

No memory

Each client’s motion guidelines, and the learnings from every review, were lost between projects.

How we solved it

We built
Motion DNA.

A motion library and MCP plugin for After Effects that supports motion designers across the whole creative process, from small, granular tasks to complete multi-step workflows.

How does it work?

Part 1 of 3

Library

Ready-made moves and easing curves: 80 moves, 14 curves, 9 styles and a kit of 159 pieces, applied as native keyframes.

Pick a move and apply it, or shape your own curve, save it and reuse it on every project.

Part 2 of 3

Brand profile

Stores a brand’s motion guidelines, its easing curves, speed benchmarks and rules, and applies them to every project.

Every review note becomes a new rule. Share the whole brand as one file.

Part 3 of 3

Agent mode

Handles technical tasks and runs workflows across other platforms and tools such as Figma, Flora or Blender.

One sentence in the chat. It does the steps in After Effects and reports the time and cost.

What’s behind the scenes?

Behind the scenes

Motion DNA is fed curated content, skills and direction. It runs on a set of motion skills and on motion references organised in a Motion Style Map, the basis for the library’s presets and styles.

20+ films

Measured frame by frame: cuts, speed, time in motion and music.

4 families

Sorted so each style can be understood and replicated.

9 styles

Ready-made looks, from Calm Modern and Elegant to Editorial, Storybook and Collage, each a mix of moves and curves.

What we fed it

Motion DNA is only as good as what it learns from. Everything here lives in the repo, so the LLM reads it in every session, and it keeps growing with every project.

2 skills

How the LLM drives it

motion-dna: the After Effects bridge, the API and the house rules. video-templates: videos written as code, rebuilt in AE.

20 films

The Motion Style Map

Measured frame by frame and by ear: 209 behaviors, fitted curves, cuts on the beat. The gaps became new curves, presets and styles.

14 curves

Motion tokens

14 curves and 6 durations that every preset asks for, so the whole library retunes in one place.

20 engines

Open source, put to work

Tone.js, react-spring, anime.js, matter.js, gl-transitions, three.js, PixiJS, Rapier, faster-whisper, FFmpeg and more.

180 resources

Outside, license checked

Scripts, expression libraries, VFX, sound effects and music, each checked before it can ship.

703 prompts

Videos made as code

Community prompts indexed by use and quality, rewritten as 15 templates on our own tokens. Never copied.

54 routes

Briefs and lessons

Routes that turn a brief into options, and a log where every review note and every fix becomes a rule.
