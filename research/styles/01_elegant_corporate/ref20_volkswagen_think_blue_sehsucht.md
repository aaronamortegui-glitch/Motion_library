## Look

## What defines it
1. One color for the whole world.
2. A camera that never stops: scenes change through travel (through objects, reflections, falling).
3. Bookend from space: push-in → pull-out.
4. Flat silhouettes in a 3D world: people as types.
5. Icon bubbles instead of words (stroke draw-on, then fill, ~10 f, no overshoot).
6. Restraint: no springs, no type until the end card.
7. Gentle clear pulse sets mood; edit independent.
8. Icons → particles → tagline, then a quiet logo.
