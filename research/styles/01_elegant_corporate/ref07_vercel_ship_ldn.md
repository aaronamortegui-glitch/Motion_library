## Music & edit
99.4 BPM, clear pulse (0.66), stable, low percussion (0.16). Cuts on beat 14.8% vs 23.2% chance (below chance), half-beat 38%: the cuts **avoid** the grid and run faster than it (median 0.72 beats).
Macro structure follows the music: quiet intro to ~3.5 s (circuit lights), louder sections from ~19.5 s and ~39.5 s, cut burst inside the loud section, music decays under the logo.

## Speed and rhythm
60.4 cuts/min, 30.5% moving; holds median 0.83 s (max 2.5 s). Cut-driven.

## Look
Near-monochrome (colorfulness 5.5): black grounds, white type, chrome 3D; rare red/blue/gradient accents; dense technical detail.

## What defines it
1. Montage of brand-system frames, each a near-still held under one beat.
2. Strict monochrome with rare single-color accents.
3. Typography shown as a system (specimens, highlight bars, particle-built words).
4. **Stepped motion** (held frames, type-on every 2 f) instead of eased tweens, even on the logo.
5. Cuts deliberately off the beat; structure follows the music only at section level.
6. A small mascot as the only humor note, bookending into the logo.
7. Logo built in place: big mark → stepped shrink → typed wordmark → long hold.
