# Cross-family patterns

Behaviors that appear in several style families. They're the most reusable entries in the ontology,
and the matching should treat them as near-universal tools whose *timing and curve* change by style.

| Pattern | Seen in | How it varies by style |
|---|---|---|
| **Logo: mark arrives first, wordmark slides out from behind it** | ref01, ref03, ref05, **ref11 Helium** (Elegant · Product UI) | Cinematic: 1.5 s with anticipation, blur/rack focus. Warm editorial: short, clean. Character: 10 f ease-in-out after a circle reveal, then icons pop |
| **Construction → solid** (outline draws on, then fills) | ref02 OpenAI (Elegant · Editorial), ref05 OpenToscana (rocket) | Editorial: geometric construction lines with handles, cut to solid. Character: simple outline draw, then fill |
| **Circular reveal (iris) from a point/dot** | ref03 (9 f @30), ref05 (5 f open, 13 f close @24) | Elegant: soft glowing disc. Character: flat solid circle |
| **A single element carries continuity across scenes** | ref02 (black dot), ref03 (coral dot), ref05 (narrator, phone, rocket → globe → logo) | Elegant: an abstract dot. Character: a character or a prop |
| **Typing** | ref02, ref03 | Editorial: fast, irregular. Warm editorial: slower with a blinking colored cursor |
| **Bookend** (opens and closes with the same mechanism or plate) | ref01, ref03, ref04 | — |
| **Ease-in push-in that flies through without landing** (accelerating zoom into the next scene) | ref13 1984 (14–18 f, Launch 0.02–0.11), ref17 Film Brazil (globe, Launch 0.087), ref20 VW (planet, Launch 0.10), ref11 Helium (zoom-through the logo) | Cinematic: into type or an eye; Character/infographic: into the globe/map; Product UI: through the logo after an anticipation |
