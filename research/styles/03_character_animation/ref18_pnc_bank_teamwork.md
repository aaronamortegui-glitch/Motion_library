## Lifecycle (from sheets, f @24)
Characters run/jump or climb into frame; cooperative acting (boosting, pulling up, handing over); physics arcs and pendulum swings (rings f160–210);
anticipation crouch before jumps (f313 → leap f327–341); celebration poses (high-five f99–108); background figures never stop. The word is never animated as type: it's revealed by the journey, then the wide cut.

## Secondary animation
Follow-through and overlapping action on limbs, pendulum swings, anticipation, idle life in the background (deer, climbers). No squash/stretch on objects or type.

## Transitions
Hard cuts letter to letter; **the drift keeps the same direction across cuts**, so motion carries over. Cut to the wide reveal, cut to the end card.

## Music & edit
136 BPM, pulse 0.51, very low percussion (0.10, melodic/acoustic bed). Cuts on beat 40% vs 32% chance, 70% on half-beats, accents 47%: a slight lean toward the beat (only 10 cuts → verdict stays "independent"). Median shot 5.1 beats.
The music builds from ~9 s and peaks around 24–26 s **with the TEAMWORK wide reveal**, then decays under the logo.

## Speed and rhythm
20 cuts/min, 73.5% moving, low speed (4.8% width/s): calm and continuous; life comes from many small actions.

## Look
Cream background; muted varied palette (teal, burgundy, orange, slate blue, olive, wood); soft shading on dimensional letters; flat-shaded elongated figures. Colorfulness 39.8.

## What defines it
1. Typography as the environment: each letter is a set the characters move through.
2. Characters carry the message through cooperative acting.
3. Constant-speed linear camera truck in every shot; cuts keep the drift direction.
4. One letter per shot, then a pull-back reveal of the whole word as payoff (on the music peak).
5. Physics-flavored secondary motion (arcs, pendulums, anticipation); no bouncy springs.
6. Living sets: holds only at the end.
7. Quiet static logo card after the reveal.
