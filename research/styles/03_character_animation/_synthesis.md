# Style 03 — Character driven · synthesis

Status: **draft from 1 reference** (ref05 OpenToscana, flat vector explainer).

## Candidate rules (to confirm with more references)
1. Characters carry the story through acting: expressions, gestures, gags.
2. Soft springs (~4% overshoot) and gentle pop-ins; energy comes from acting, not from fast curves.
3. One saturated background color per section, changing with the topic.
4. Sets assemble themselves prop by prop.
5. Visual gags as punctuation.
6. Icons and speech bubbles instead of text; minimal typography.
7. Continuity devices: a recurring narrator, objects that travel between scenes.
