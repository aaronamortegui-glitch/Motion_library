# Style 04 — Mixed media / collage · synthesis (PROPOSED family)

Status: **proposed from 1 reference** (ref04, health care explainer). Needs Gian's approval and more references.

Why a new family: ref04 breaks the core Elegant rules (restraint, holds, restricted palette, clean landings),
is not playful (serious editorial explainer), and has no acting characters (silhouettes and crowds only).

## Candidate rules (to confirm with more references)
1. Collage of photo cutouts + textures; every layer is textured.
2. 2.5D camera travel through layers replaces cuts.
3. Smooth motion on ones + texture boil on threes ("two clocks").
4. Never still: layered loops fill every hold.
5. Neon accents on a desaturated base.
6. Stencil / label typography with glitch builds.
7. Energy from density and texture, not from springs.

## Measured so far
| Behavior | Duration | Curve |
|---|---|---|
| Camera travel between scenes | 0.6–1.5 s (up to 4.5 s) | ~linear or ease-in-out (Flat / Launch / Cruise) |
| Texture boil | every 3 f @30 | stepped |
| Countdown digits | ~0.4–0.5 s per number | stepped + flicker |
