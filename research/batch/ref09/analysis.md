## Elements
Typography (kanji, sliced Latin), generative/fractal 3D, HUD graphics (timecode, BPM readout, chapter labels), manga vocabulary.

## Measured
- Mandala ring growth f82–105: perfectly linear (Flat, 0.004).
- Split-word cards exactly **10 f apart** (0.76 beat → dotted-eighth feel).
- Type-on ~4 f per character.

## Secondary animation
Glitch slicing, strobes, scan lines, HUD counters always running.

## Transitions
Hard cuts, glitch slicing, strobes, white flashes, frame inversions.

## Music & edit
137 BPM (the HUD even prints "137.14 BPM"), clear pulse (0.61). The montage **starts on a beat** (offset -0.05 s) and runs **syncopated** cut patterns:
measured cuts land on the beat only 24% vs 32% by chance because they fall on dotted/off-beat subdivisions (cards every 0.76 beat).
The build (0–12.4 s) has no cuts: flow while the track builds, then the montage on the drop.

## Look
Vermilion / black / white with a cyan accent. Colorfulness 39.

## What defines it
1. Two-speed structure: continuous build → montage on the drop.
2. Persistent HUD frame carries continuity across cuts.
3. Kanji filling the frame and sliced into strips.
4. Manga visual vocabulary (speed lines, SFX katakana).
5. Generative / fractal 3D.
6. Syncopated cut runs (off-beat subdivisions), not cuts on every beat.
